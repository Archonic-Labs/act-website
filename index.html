How it works

  1. 1

    Start Actually Broadcast

    Tap record on the home screen once. Then go back to whatever you were watching.

  2. 2

    Double-tap the back

    When something sounds off, tap the back of your iPhone twice. Actually takes the last few seconds of screen and audio.

  3. 3

    Read the verdict

    It finds the claim, checks it against sources on the web, and shows the answer without stopping the video.

Dynamic Island

The verdict comes to you.

The check runs while the video keeps playing. The claim, a one-line answer, and its sources show up in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen.

“The Great Wall of China is the only man-made thing you can see from space.”
